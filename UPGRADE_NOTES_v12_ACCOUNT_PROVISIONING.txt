KAFUBU BLOCK SCHOOL PORTAL - VERSION 12

ACCOUNT PROVISIONING FIX

Production databases created by version 11 could contain only the Headteacher
account. Version 12 safely creates any missing official Deputy Headteacher, HR,
Guidance and HOD accounts as inactive accounts with random passwords.

After deployment:
1. Log in as Headteacher.
2. Open Manage Passwords.
3. Search for "hod" to display all HOD accounts.
4. Reset each account with a strong private temporary password.
5. Password reset automatically activates the account.
6. Each HOD must change the temporary password after the first login.

No known or public default passwords are enabled in production.
Existing school records on the persistent disk are preserved.
