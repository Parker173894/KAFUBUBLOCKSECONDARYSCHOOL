KAFUBU BLOCK SCHOOL PORTAL - VERSION 16

RESULTS AND ATTENDANCE UPGRADE

1. Test 1 and Test 2 have been removed from result entry and display.
2. Results now use Midterm Test and End-of-Term Test.
3. The result average is calculated automatically from the two assessments.
4. Grade 10, Grade 11 and Grade 12 use the 1-9 Distinction/Merit/Credit/Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory grading scale.
5. Form 1, Form 2, Form 3 and Form 4 use Outstanding/Advanced/Basic/Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory grading.
6. Teacher comments are automatic:
   75-100 Excellent performance
   50-74 Good performance
   40-49 Can do better
   0-39 Fail
7. Class teachers can mark pupils Present or Absent by date, term and academic year.
8. Attendance Present and Absent totals appear in online results and pupil PDF reports.
9. Existing result records are migrated safely when the new midterm field is created.
10. Existing pupil, staff, result and upload records on /var/data are preserved.

INSTALLATION

- Upload all files inside kafubu_security_backup_upgrade to the GitHub repository root.
- Replace files with the same names.
- Do not upload or replace school.db.
- On Render use Manual Deploy > Clear build cache & deploy.
