KAFUBU BLOCK SCHOOL PORTAL - VERSION 13

RESULT MANAGEMENT UPGRADE

- Results are displayed separately under Term 1, Term 2 and Term 3.
- Grade and term filters can be used together.
- HR, HODs, Headteacher and Deputy Headteacher can edit or delete results.
- Existing duplicate results are labelled, and future duplicates are blocked.
- Teachers can enter results but cannot edit or delete them after submission.
- Existing records in /var/data/school.db are preserved.
